# Screenly Wallpaper Credits & Provenance

Every bundled wallpaper is **original procedural artwork** generated by `scripts/generate_wallpapers.py` (v1) and `scripts/generate_wallpapers_v2.py` (v2, numpy) with fixed random seeds. **No third-party images, stock assets, AI-image models, Apple wallpapers or Recordly wallpapers are included.** Names such as "Carina Nebula" are evocative labels, not depictions of real objects.

License: original work created for the Screenly project, distributed under the same terms as the Screenly application unless the owner states otherwise. Nothing here requires third-party attribution.

Resolution column is measured from the files (PIL) at the time of writing; all are landscape 3840x2160.

## Removed

The 39 legacy files inherited from Recordly (names such as `tahoe-light.jpg`, `sequoia-blue.jpg`, `sonoma-*.jpg`, `ventura*.jpg`, `wallpaper*.jpg`, `wispysky.mp4`) were deleted from the bundle on 2026-09-29. Old projects that reference them fall back to the default wallpaper `aurora-borealis-polar.jpg`.

## Known limitations

- These are stylised procedural images, not photographs. Photographic Alpine/Coast/Botanical sets from Poly Haven / Wikimedia Commons / NASA were not added because no per-image license verification was performed.
- `topographic-light-relief`, `minimal-paper-fiber` and `minimal-graphite-texture` are intentionally near-flat and low-contrast.
